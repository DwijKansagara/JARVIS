Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Cookies

JARVIS does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The anonymous visit and appreciation counters do not set cookies or write an identifier to browser storage.

Optional local storage

When “Remember on this device” is enabled, the app uses browser storage for the OpenRouter key and chat history you choose to retain. This is functional storage requested by you. Disable the option or use Disconnect to remove it.

Consent banner

No optional tracking is currently loaded, so a tracking-consent banner is not shown. If advertising, analytics, or non-essential embeds are added, they should remain blocked until any required consent is obtained.
